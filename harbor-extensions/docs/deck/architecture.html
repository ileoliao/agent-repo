Read it left to right. Two kinds of callers: engineers, through small tools in the Harbor portal, and AI agents such as Copilot, through the Harbor CLI. Both pass the same four checks in Harbor: who are you, may you do this, pass the call on with your identity attached, and record it. Behind the gate are our systems: Harbor itself, Jira, ServiceNow, GitLab, and services our teams build themselves, like the outage management service. Three properties keep it safe and cheap: tools are sandboxed and never hold credentials, each system is integrated once, and the layer holds no business data.
